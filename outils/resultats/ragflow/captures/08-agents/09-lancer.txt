Quel est le plafond des pénalités de retard du contrat Batterix ?

Answer

According to the documentation, the penalty for delay in the Batterix supply contract is 0.5% of the order amount per day of delay, capped at 8% of the order amount.

This information is found in the document titled "CONTRATD'APPROVISIONNEMENTN°BX-2025-117" (Article 6).
